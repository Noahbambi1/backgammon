Rules implemented

  • Opening roll (one die each, higher starts; ties re-roll).
  • Both dice must be played when possible; if only one can be, the higher die must be used. Doubles play four times.
  • Hitting, entering from the bar, bearing off (exact or with a higher die when no checker is farther back).
  • Doubling cube with ownership, take/pass, redoubles up to 64. Crawford rule in match play.
  • Gammon (×2) and backgammon (×3) scoring, match play to 1/3/5/7 points or unlimited.
  • Undo within your turn, resign, pip counts, move highlighting.
