## Description
Backgammon the way it's actually played: full rules, the doubling cube, Crawford rule, gammons and backgammons, match play to 1, 3, 5 or 7 points — and four ways to play.

PLAY VS BOT
Three levels. Easy just moves; Medium plays sound positional backgammon; Hard looks two rolls ahead across every dice combination and knows when to double and when to take.

TWO PLAYERS, ONE PHONE
Enter two names and pass the phone. The board can rotate for whoever is on move.

PLAY ONLINE
Create a room, share the six-letter code (or a link, or a QR code) and play a friend anywhere. No account, no sign-up, no ads.

NEARBY — NO INTERNET
On a plane? One phone turns on its hotspot, the other joins it, scan each other's code and play — completely offline.

THE DETAILS
• Tap to see legal moves or drag your checkers
• Play a whole roll in one tap — the checker hops through each point
• Proper opening roll: each player rolls, the winner may play it or roll again
• Undo within a turn, resign, pip counts, match history
• Dice from your device's cryptographic random generator, with statistics you can check
• Landscape and portrait, phones and tablets
• Wooden board sounds, mute button
• Nothing collected, nothing tracked, works without an account
